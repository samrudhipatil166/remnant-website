Frequently asked questions

What is Remnant?

Remnant is an iOS app for leaving voice notes pinned to real-world locations. Record a message, and it stays exactly where you left it — for you to revisit, or for a stranger to discover if they happen to walk by.

Is Remnant free to use?

Yes, Remnant is free to download and use.

How close do I need to be to discover someone else's Remnant?

You'll be notified once you're within about 0.1 miles of a Remnant someone else has left nearby.

Is my exact location shared with other people?

No. A nearby Remnant's position stays approximate on the map until you're close enough to reveal it, and your own exact location is never shown to other users.

Can I delete a Remnant I've left?

Yes, you can permanently delete any Remnant you've created from its detail screen at any time.

Is Remnant available on Android?

Not yet — Remnant is currently in beta on iOS via TestFlight, with a full App Store release coming soon.
