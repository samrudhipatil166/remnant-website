Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Remnant ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is a location-based voice note app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Remnant, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information. When you create an account, we collect basic identifying information (such as email address or phone number) through our authentication provider, Firebase Authentication.

Location Data. Remnant's core feature requires access to your device's GPS location. We collect:

  • Your location when you create ("drop") a voice note, in order to pin it to that location
  • Your location when you attempt to unlock a voice note, in order to verify physical proximity to the pinned location

We do not track or store your location continuously or in the background. Location is only accessed when you actively use location-dependent features of the App.

Voice Note Content. We collect and store the audio recordings you create, along with associated metadata (timestamp, location, and any text you add).

Date of Birth. We collect your date of birth at signup to verify you meet the App's minimum age requirement of 16. Your date of birth is stored on your account record for as long as your account is active, so that age eligibility can be re-verified if needed.

Usage and Device Information. We may collect standard technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, and crash/diagnostic logs to help us maintain and improve the App.

Inviting Friends. Remnant may let you invite friends to download the App (for example, by sharing a link to the App Store listing). Inviting a friend does not grant them access to your voice notes, account, or any other data — it simply shares information about the App itself.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate core App functionality (pinning and unlocking voice notes based on location)
  • Authenticate your account and secure your data
  • Enforce our minimum age requirement
  • Maintain, debug, and improve the App
  • Communicate with you about the App (e.g., important updates)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your voice note content or location history for advertising.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Firebase (a Google service) for authentication, data storage, and hosting. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google's privacy and security terms.
  • With other users, at your direction: Voice notes are private and not visible to other users unless they physically arrive at the pinned location, if you have made that note discoverable.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers: If Remnant is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not share your precise location history with other users. Other users can only learn that a note exists at a location by physically being at that location.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information and voice notes for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual voice notes or your entire account at any time within the App. When you delete your account, we immediately delete your account settings, your voice notes (including their audio and photo files), and your account credentials. This action cannot be undone. A limited technical record may be retained after account deletion — for example, another user's record that they discovered one of your notes — solely to help us respond to support inquiries. This record does not include your voice note content and cannot be used to re-create your account or content. We periodically review and clean up this residual data.

5. Children's Privacy

Remnant is not intended for use by anyone under 16. We verify this at signup via date of birth, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under this age. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 16, we will delete their account and associated data.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for location access (via your device settings — note this will disable core App features)
  • Export your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@remnant-app.com.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including Firebase's built-in authentication and access-control rules, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Location Permissions

You can enable or disable location permissions for Remnant at any time in your device's Settings app. Disabling location access will prevent you from dropping or unlocking voice notes, as this is core to how the App works.

9. Third-Party Services

Remnant uses the following third-party service:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): authentication, database, and storage. See Google's Privacy Policy.
