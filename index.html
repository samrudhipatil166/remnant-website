Questions

Is Remnant a journaling app?

Yes — Remnant is a location-based journaling app. Instead of writing entries, you record a voice note and pin it to the exact place it happened, so your journal is mapped to the world rather than sorted by date alone.

What is an audio diary, and how does Remnant work as one?

An audio diary lets you record your thoughts as voice notes instead of text. Remnant works as a geotagged voice diary — each entry is tied to a physical location, so revisiting a place can surface the memory you left there.

Can I use Remnant as a voice time capsule?

Yes. Any voice note you leave stays pinned to its location indefinitely — you can replay it yourself anytime, and if you allow it, someone else can discover it by walking to that exact spot. It functions as a voice time capsule tied to place rather than a set unlock date.

Is Remnant good for travel journaling?

Remnant works well as a travel journal app since every voice note is automatically organized by the place you recorded it. Looking back at a trip means revisiting the map, not scrolling a feed.

How is Remnant different from a regular voice memo app?

A standard voice memo app just stores recordings by date. Remnant ties each voice note to the place it was recorded — you can replay your own anytime, but discovering someone else's for the first time still means walking to that exact spot.

Can I connect with other people through Remnant?

Not directly — Remnant isn't a chat or social app. There's no messaging, profiles, or replies. What you get instead is a quiet, one-way moment: a stranger's voice, left exactly where they stood, that you happen to find by walking by. It's less about connecting with someone and more about feeling like you're not alone in a place.

Does Remnant cost anything?

No — Remnant is free forever, with no ads and no subscription.
